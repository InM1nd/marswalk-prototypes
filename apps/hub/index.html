DACH case study · Gamewheel + independent write-ups

Build vs buy

  • Outgrow $14–25/mo entry, jumps to $600/mo (Business tier) just to remove Outgrow branding and use a custom domain. Public pricing, subject to change.
  • Wheelio From ~$14.92/mo, Shopify-only. White-label and A/B testing gated behind higher tiers. Public pricing, subject to change.
  • DACH competitors Real market: Gamewheel (Berlin, founded 2014) is a direct competitor — playable-ad/gamification agency + platform, 3000+ playable ads claimed, 40% CTR (their own figure, unaudited). Plus an established "Glücksrad mieten" niche of trade-show wheel-rental agencies (HAPPY Marketing Solutions, event-spiel.de, and others) — mostly physical/hybrid, tablet-controlled, generic templates. Our edge is owning a configurable code engine, not "nobody does this here."
  • 3D / WebGL space Less crowded — specialized vendors exist (Threekit, Danthree Studio, ConfigureKit, Nexgits) but they build for e-commerce configurators, not social/ad agencies. The real differentiation gap sits here, not in wheel/scratch.

Off-the-shelf 2D tools charge monthly and still show their brand at low tiers — our edge there is custom branding with no rent from day one, not a new mechanic. The 3D formats (configurator, quiz, plinko) are where the market is genuinely thinner and the wow-factor argument is strongest.

Delivery & domains

  • Default: our subdomain clientname.marswalk.media — zero coordination, live immediately, we control it end to end. Right default for fast-turnaround activations (TikTok in-feed, QR at a booth) where waiting on client IT isn't an option.
  • Upgrade: client's subdomain promo.clientbrand.com — one CNAME record from their side, inherits their brand trust. Worth the coordination delay for bigger retainer clients or planned campaigns, not for quick activations.
  • Skip: one-off purchased domains Buying a new domain per campaign is recurring cost and setup for no real benefit here — domain age mostly matters for email/SMS deliverability, not ad-platform landing pages. Reserve it for the rare campaign with its own standalone brand identity.
  • Never: raw *.vercel.app links Fine for showing a draft to the client before launch. Not for production ads — ad platforms often flag shared platform domains, and there's no first-party tracking. Same build either way, just swap the domain alias in Vercel.
