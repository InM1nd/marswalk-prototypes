Runner — characters & props

The runner isn't tied to a scooter. Each campaign picks a hero, two obstacles and a collectible that fit the brand. Every hero shares the same hitbox and physics, so swapping the art never changes the difficulty, the scoring or the prize flow.

A hero for every vertical

Eight ready-made casts. The obstacles carry the brand's story (fees to dodge, delays to skip, potholes to hop) and the collectible is the brand's reward (cashback, tickets, ride credits).

A world for every hero

Each cast comes with its own map: the same four parallax layers (sky, far, mid, ground), so the engine never changes, only the art. The hero and props from above drop straight in.

Same hero, any style

One character rig with eight skins. The art direction is a campaign setting, not a rebuild.

These are static concept sketches (SVG). In the live game each hero gets run, jump, double-jump and crash poses plus a squash-and-stretch on landing. Brand colours, logo and copy come from the campaign config.
